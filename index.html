Statutory Discount Rules (RA 9994 / RA 10754 / RA 11314)

  • 20% Fare Discount: Applies to Students (with valid school ID), Senior Citizens, and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) on regular/shared rides.
  • Pakyawan / Special Trips: Capped per trip per local ordinance; divided equally among riding passengers.
  • Sample Data Notice: Figures below are demonstration entries modeled on Nueva Ecija municipal matrices. Always check the laminated LGU taripa inside the vehicle.
